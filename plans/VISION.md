# **AthleteOS**

## **Product Vision Proposal**

### **Reimagining Athlete Performance Management**

### **Executive Summary**

AthleteOS is building the next generation of athlete performance software, starting with competitive swimming and designed to expand across high-performance sport.

Our ambition is not simply to build another coaching platform. Our ambition is to build the world’s first AI-native operating system for athlete performance.

Current coaching platforms provide excellent tools for storing workouts, analysing results and managing athletes. However, coaches still spend significant time entering data, switching between applications and searching for information instead of coaching athletes.

AthleteOS will deliver the same comprehensive capabilities expected from a modern performance platform while fundamentally changing how coaches interact with technology.

Our philosophy is simple:

**Less administration. Better collaboration. Smarter coaching.**

---

# **The Problem**

Every day, coaches create valuable information.

* Training sessions  
* Athlete times  
* Race videos  
* Technique observations  
* Wellness data  
* Strength & conditioning results  
* Physiotherapy updates  
* Nutrition plans  
* Competition performances

Unfortunately, much of this information is either lost, duplicated or stored across multiple disconnected systems.

As a result:

* Coaches spend hours completing administration.  
* Support staff work independently.  
* Valuable athlete insights are missed.  
* Decisions are often based on incomplete information.  
* Technology creates more work instead of reducing it.

---

# **Our Vision**

AthleteOS becomes the single source of truth for every athlete.

Every athlete has one evolving **AthleteOS360™** profile where every training session, race, observation, wellness check, support staff note and performance metric is connected.

Rather than simply recording information, AthleteOS understands it, organises it and transforms it into meaningful coaching insights.

---

# **Our Philosophy**

Every feature must satisfy at least one of three objectives:

* Save coaches time.  
* Improve collaboration.  
* Help someone make a better coaching decision.

If it doesn’t achieve one of these outcomes, it doesn’t belong in the product.

---

# **Our Competitive Strategy**

We acknowledge that outstanding coaching platforms already exist.

Rather than competing by offering fewer features or simply lower pricing, AthleteOS will compete by delivering a dramatically better coaching experience.

Our strategy is straightforward:

**Everything existing platforms do. Significantly faster. Significantly smarter.**

# **Our Biggest Differentiator**

Our goal is not to have more features.

Our goal is to create the fastest coaching workflow available.

Examples include:

* Photograph a whiteboard and instantly generate a structured workout.  
* Speak attendance while walking the pool deck.  
* Record athlete times using voice.  
* Dictate coach observations without stopping the session.  
* Upload race videos and automatically receive splits, stroke rate and technique analysis.  
* Ask AI questions instead of searching through reports.

Every interaction is designed to minimise time spent behind a screen and maximise time spent coaching athletes.

---

# **Design Principles**

Every feature should follow these principles:

* Never type if you can speak.  
* Never search if you can ask.  
* Capture information once.  
* Use it forever.  
* Keep coaches on pool deck—not behind keyboards.  
* AI works quietly in the background.  
* Every athlete has one source of truth.

---

# **Long-Term Vision**

Competitive swimming is our first market.

The AthleteOS platform is being designed from the ground up to support multiple sports including:

* Triathlon  
* Athletics  
* Cycling  
* Rowing  
* Surf Lifesaving  
* Team Sports

By building a flexible athlete management platform rather than a swimming-specific application, AthleteOS has the opportunity to become the operating system for athlete performance across sport.

---

# **Our Promise**

We are not building software that simply stores information.

We are building software that helps coaches become better coaches.

Every interaction with AthleteOS should save time, improve collaboration or support a better coaching decision.
